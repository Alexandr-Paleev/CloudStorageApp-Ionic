Vehicle: 2021 Seat Leon 1.5 TSI, registration 4821 LMK
Period of cover: 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027

TYPE OF COVER

Comprehensive cover, including third-party liability, fire and theft, accidental damage to your own vehicle, windscreen and glass, and roadside assistance.

EXCESS

The compulsory excess for any claim for damage to your own vehicle is 300 euros. Windscreen repair has no excess; windscreen replacement has an excess of 75 euros. Claims for theft have an excess of 500 euros.

PREMIUM

The annual premium is 486 euros, paid in twelve monthly instalments of 40.50 euros on the 1st of each month.

NAMED DRIVERS

Andrei Kotov (main driver) and Elena Kotova. Any other person driving the vehicle is covered for third-party liability only.

ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE

Available 24 hours a day anywhere in Spain, Portugal and France, from kilometre zero. Call 900 300 811. Towing up to 150 km to the nearest authorised garage is included. A replacement car is provided for up to 5 days if the repair takes longer than 48 hours.

MAKING A CLAIM

Report any accident within 7 days by phone or through the Northwind app. Take photographs of the damage and fill in the joint accident report (parte amistoso) with the other driver when possible.

RENEWAL

The policy renews automatically on 1 March each year unless you tell us otherwise at least one month before the renewal date. We will send the new premium 45 days before renewal.

NO-CLAIMS BONUS

Your current no-claims discount is 40%, for 6 years without a claim.
