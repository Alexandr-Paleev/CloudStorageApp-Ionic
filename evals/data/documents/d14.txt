NETLINK HX300 WI-FI ROUTER — QUICK START GUIDE

IN THE BOX

The router, a power adapter (12 V, 1.5 A), one Ethernet cable and this guide.

CONNECTING

1. Connect the fibre terminal (ONT) to the yellow WAN port with the Ethernet cable.
2. Plug in the power adapter and press the power button on the back.
3. Wait about two minutes until the Internet light turns solid white.

DEFAULT SETTINGS

The default Wi-Fi network names and password are printed on the label under the router. The 2.4 GHz network ends in -24 and the 5 GHz network ends in -5G.

To open the settings page, connect to the router and go to http://192.168.1.1 in a browser. The default username is admin and the default password is the one printed on the label as "Admin key". You will be asked to change it on the first login.

CHANGING THE WI-FI PASSWORD

In the settings page, open Wireless → Security, type the new password (at least 8 characters) and press Save. Every device will need the new password to reconnect.

RESETTING TO FACTORY SETTINGS

If you have forgotten the admin password, hold the Reset button on the back for 10 seconds with a paper clip, until all lights flash. The router restarts with the settings on the label. Your Wi-Fi names and passwords will be lost.

LIGHTS

Power solid white: the router is on.
Internet solid white: connected. Blinking orange: no connection to the fibre terminal. Solid orange: connected to the terminal but no Internet; call your provider.
Wi-Fi blinking: data is being sent.

GUEST NETWORK

A separate guest network can be switched on in Wireless → Guest. Guests get Internet access but cannot see devices on your home network.
