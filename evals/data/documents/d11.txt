PULSE FITNESS CLUB — MEMBERSHIP TERMS

Member: Andrei Kotov
Membership number: PF-208877
Plan: Full Access, 12-month commitment
Start date: 15 January 2026

FEES

The monthly fee is 44.90 euros, charged by direct debit on the 1st of each month. A one-off joining fee of 30 euros was paid on sign-up. The fee includes the gym floor, all group classes, the pool and the sauna. Personal training sessions are not included and cost 35 euros each.

OPENING HOURS

Monday to Friday 6:30 to 23:00, Saturday and Sunday 8:00 to 21:00. The pool closes 30 minutes before the club. The club is closed on 25 December and 1 January.

GUESTS

Full Access members may bring one guest per month free of charge. Further guest passes cost 10 euros per visit.

FREEZING THE MEMBERSHIP

The membership can be frozen for one to three months per year, for a fee of 5 euros per month, by asking at reception at least 7 days before the freeze starts. The commitment period is extended by the months frozen.

CANCELLATION

After the 12-month commitment, the membership continues month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice, by email to members@pulsefitness.example or at reception. Cancelling during the commitment period costs 50% of the remaining monthly fees.

CLASS BOOKINGS

Classes are booked in the app up to 48 hours in advance. A booking cancelled less than 2 hours before the class, or a missed class, counts as a no-show; three no-shows in a month block bookings for 7 days.

LOCKERS

Day lockers are free. Monthly lockers cost 8 euros. Items left in day lockers after closing are removed and kept for 14 days.
