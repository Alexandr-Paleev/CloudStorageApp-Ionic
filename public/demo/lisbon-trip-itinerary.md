# Lisbon, 14–18 May 2026

A sample itinerary for the demo; the bookings are made up.

- **Flight out:** EX 101 (sample), Madrid → Lisbon, departs 08:35, arrives 08:55 local time.
- **Hotel:** Casa do Exemplo, Alfama. Check-in from 15:00, four nights, breakfast included.
- **Day 1:** walk through Alfama, Miradouro de Santa Luzia, dinner with fado.
- **Day 2:** tram 28 to the castle, then Time Out Market.
- **Day 3:** train from Rossio to Sintra (40 minutes); Pena Palace tickets for 11:00.
- **Day 4:** Belém: the tower, the monastery, pastéis de nata.
- **Budget:** about 650 EUR for hotel, food and transport.
- **Flight back:** EX 102 (sample), departs 19:10.
